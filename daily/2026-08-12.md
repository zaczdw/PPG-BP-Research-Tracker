## Few-Shot Personalized Blood Pressure Estimation from Photoplethysmography and Physiological Priors via Low-Rank Adaptation

- 来源：[IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41701592/)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、personalization、LoRA、physiological prior
- 数据集：UCI Cuffless BP、University of Queensland、CAS-BP
- 输入：PPG、physiological priors
- 方法：Population-pretrained Transformer personalized with sampling-rate-robust LoRA (SRR-LoRA) and pulse-pressure consistency loss.
- 主要结果：Reported SBP MAE 1.52 mmHg and DBP MAE 1.07 mmHg after few-shot personalization; 2,405 pretraining subjects and 316 personalization subjects were described in the prior daily review.
- 校准：few-shot personal fine-tuning；Requires labeled personal PPG/BP examples; exact shot count varies by experiment and is not safely reduced here to a single number.
- Subject split：reported-subject-aware；Personalization cohorts are subject-based; exact train/validation/test protocol still needs full-text table verification.
- External validation：false；Multiple datasets used, but no clearly independent external clinical validation was confirmed.
- 可部署性：有条件可部署（conditional）
- 推理输入：PPG、stored personalized LoRA parameters
- 泄露风险：medium；No evidence that current test BP is needed at inference; personalization can still make same-person evaluation optimistic if calibration/test windows are too close.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## Feasibility and performance evaluation of PPG on a Galaxy Watch in continuous central blood pressure monitoring

- 来源：[European Heart Journal – Digital Health](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12912915/)
- 主题：PPG、Galaxy Watch、central BP、change tracking、external prospective validation
- 数据集：6117 measurements/440 development participants、114 catheterization validation participants
- 输入：Galaxy Watch 6 PPG、optional baseline calibration
- 方法：PPG algorithm detects relative MAP/SBP/DBP changes against invasive central aortic BP; prospective catheterization evaluation.
- 主要结果：For ≥15% MAP increase AUC 0.85; MAP-change correlation r=0.92 with bias 0.51 mmHg; median detection delay 1 min; uncalibrated change detection remained robust.
- 校准：baseline calibration optional for change tracking；Calibrated and uncalibrated relative-change analyses were both reported.
- Subject split：true；Development and prospective evaluation cohorts are separate.
- External validation：true；Reference is invasive central aortic BP.
- 可部署性：适合 ΔBP/MAP 变化检测，非绝对 BP 产品验证（promising-change-tracking）
- 推理输入：wrist PPG、optional baseline
- 泄露风险：low；Reference BP defines outcomes but is not needed by deployed PPG model.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## Validation and Subgroup Analysis of the Accuracy of the Photoplethysmography-based Microlife Cuffless Upper-arm Wearable Blood Pressure Monitor

- 来源：[American Journal of Hypertension](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41390956/)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、clinical validation、skin tone、age
- 数据集：prospective 120-participant validation cohort
- 输入：upper-arm PPG、stored cuff calibration
- 方法：Commercial upper-arm PPG cuffless monitor evaluated with sequential same-arm measurements under AAMI/ESH/ISO 81060-2:2018/Amd 1:2020.
- 主要结果：120 adults; met AAMI/ESH/ISO accuracy criteria and showed broadly consistent results across age and Fitzpatrick skin-tone groups.
- 校准：individual cuff calibration；Calibrated against a clinically validated mercury sphygmomanometer.
- Subject split：not applicable to device validation；Prospective measurement validation rather than model train/test split.
- External validation：true；General adult population with subgroup analysis.
- 可部署性：临床验证较强但需要袖带校准（strong-conditional）
- 推理输入：upper-arm PPG、stored personal calibration
- 泄露风险：low；Calibration is an authorized deployment input, not leakage, provided later reference readings are only evaluation targets.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
