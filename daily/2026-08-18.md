## Long-term accuracy and stability of blood pressure measurements from a smartwatch: Prospective validation study

- 来源：[Digital Health](https://doi.org/10.1177/20552076261415923)
- 主题：PPG、smartwatch、cuffless BP、28-day calibration stability
- 数据集：37-participant 28-day prospective cohort
- 输入：Samsung Galaxy Watch 5 PPG pipeline、stored Day-0 cuff calibration
- 方法：Day-0 calibration followed by 27 days of paired Galaxy Watch 5 and Omron M4 measurements.
- 主要结果：Overall MD about −0.34 mmHg SBP and +0.62 mmHg DBP; estimated 28-day drift −0.19/+1.02 mmHg. Error increased when actual BP moved about 10 mmHg away from the initial calibration point.
- 校准：single onboarding cuff calibration per manufacturer workflow；Prospective longitudinal evaluation of calibration stability.
- Subject split：not applicable；Device validation cohort, not a retrained ML split.
- External validation：true；Small, mostly young/normotensive cohort limits generalizability.
- 可部署性：真实 28 天可用证据，但人群有限（strong-conditional）
- 推理输入：smartwatch PPG、stored Day-0 calibration
- 泄露风险：low；Validates stability, not universal accuracy across hypertensive BP ranges.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
