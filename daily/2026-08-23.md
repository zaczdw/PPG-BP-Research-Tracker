## Evaluation of Alysis-001 cuffless blood pressure estimation algorithm against the European Society of Hypertension awake/asleep test criteria in hypertensive patients

- 来源：[Hypertension Research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41440-026-02728-z)
- 主题：cuffless BP、wearable、clinical validation、ESH awake/asleep、calibration-free
- 数据集：independent development cohorts: 953 seated-rest + 150 treadmill、prospective 24 h hypertensive cohort
- 输入：green/red/IR PPG、electro-potential pulse signal、accelerometer、gyroscope、skin temperature、age、height、weight
- 方法：Biomechanical Moens–Korteweg-based multiparametric algorithm estimates velocity, volume and arterial elasticity; motion/posture correction and signal-quality rejection are applied.
- 主要结果：92 enrolled, 78 analyzed; passed all ESH awake/asleep criteria under both extraction strategies; ICC >0.70, non-dipper sensitivity 0.93, specificity 0.75, accuracy 0.82.
- 校准：demographic calibration only；Uses age, height and weight; no initial or periodic cuff calibration.
- Subject split：true；Evaluation cohort has no overlap with independent development datasets.
- External validation：true；Reference ABPM every 30 min; ESH awake/asleep primary test, not the entire cuffless-device validation suite.
- 可部署性：真实可穿戴证据较强（strong-conditional）
- 推理输入：multispectral PPG、electro-potential、IMU、temperature、demographics
- 泄露风险：low；Reference ABPM is used for validation, not fed into Alysis estimates.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
