## Change Point–Aware Evaluation and Re-Calibration of PPG-Based Blood Pressure Estimation

- 来源：[MLHC 2026 / arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18639)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、change point、recalibration、evaluation
- 数据集：not_reported
- 输入：PPG、BP trajectory for change-point evaluation
- 方法：Detects distributional change points in BP trajectories, evaluates models around fluctuations, and triggers targeted recalibration.
- 主要结果：Shows substantial model degradation near BP change points; periodic calibration is insufficient, while change-point-triggered recalibration improves robustness (exact values pending table extraction).
- 校准：change-point-triggered；Deployment validity depends on whether trigger detection uses predicted BP/PPG only or true reference BP.
- Subject split：unknown；Full evaluation protocol requires audit.
- External validation：false；No independent clinical external validation confirmed.
- 可部署性：触发机制需改造成无参考 BP（conceptual/conditional）
- 推理输入：PPG、change trigger available at deployment
- 泄露风险：high；Valuable evaluation framework; its deployment claim must separate oracle change points from detectable real-world triggers.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
