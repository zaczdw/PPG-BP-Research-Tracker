## The role of dataset integrity, calibration and signal quality in neural network-based blood pressure estimation

- 来源：[Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-66882-7)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、dataset integrity、calibration、signal synchronization、data leakage
- 数据集：curated MIMIC-III、curated VitalDB
- 输入：PPG、ECG-derived features for one baseline
- 方法：MLP, ResNet and TCN are compared on datasets differing in synchronization; subject-wise splits and 3–9 initial reference measurements test calibration effects.
- 主要结果：VitalDB consistently outperformed MIMIC-III; calibration gains diminished beyond three measurements; calibrated DBP reached numerical clinical limits while SBP remained difficult.
- 校准：initial subject-specific reference BP；Diminishing return beyond three initial measurements.
- Subject split：true；Abstract explicitly reports subject-wise splits to prevent leakage.
- External validation：false；Cross-dataset comparison is not the same as training on one and untouched external testing on another.
- 可部署性：有条件可部署（conditional）
- 推理输入：PPG、possibly ECG/PAT features depending model、stored calibration
- 泄露风险：low-to-medium；A methodological audit paper; still requires checking how curated test windows were selected.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
