## CardioState-JEPA: Delay-Aware Cross-Modal Learning of a Shared Cardiac Representation

- 来源：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12944)
- 主题：PPG、ECG、PCG、foundation model、JEPA、delay alignment
- 数据集：multiple unimodal and paired cardiac datasets
- 输入：PPG or ECG or PCG
- 方法：Two-stage joint-embedding predictive architecture with modality tokenizers, shared Transformer, masked latent prediction, and learned cross-modal delay alignment.
- 主要结果：Frozen encoder reports +8.2 AUROC points for PPG classification, +18.8 for PCG murmur detection and +15.5 for ECG classification over the best SSL baseline.
- 校准：not applicable；No cuffless BP calibration study.
- Subject split：task-specific；Downstream protocols vary.
- External validation：true；Not a BP-regression validation.
- 可部署性：可作为 PPG encoder（research-enabler）
- 推理输入：one available cardiac modality, e.g. PPG
- 泄露风险：low for BP target leakage；No absolute BP claim should be inferred.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
