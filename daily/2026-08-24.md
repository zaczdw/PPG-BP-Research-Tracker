## A Multi-posture Asymmetry-aware Intelligent Bilateral Observation Dataset for Cardiovascular Monitoring

- 来源：[Scientific Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07915-8)
- 主题：PPG、dataset、ring、bilateral、posture、cuffless BP
- 数据集：MAIBO
- 输入：left ring PPG、right ring PPG、posture、age、sex、BMI
- 方法：Dataset descriptor: synchronized bilateral ring PPG across three standardized postures with SBP/DBP/HR and demographic labels.
- 主要结果：1,810 participants and 7,478 sessions; designed for bilateral and posture-dependent cardiovascular benchmarking.
- 校准：not applicable to dataset；Reference BP labels are provided; downstream users must define calibration protocol.
- Subject split：not imposed；Dataset users must split by participant ID; random session/window split is unsafe.
- External validation：false；Dataset resource, not an externally validated estimator.
- 可部署性：贴近 ring wearable（research-enabler）
- 推理输入：ring PPG、optional posture/IMU
- 泄露风险：low at dataset construction / high if mis-split；Dataset does not guarantee safe downstream splitting by itself.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## HealthRing: Physiology Dataset for Health Sensing on Rings

- 来源：[Scientific Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07289-x)
- 主题：PPG、dataset、ring、motion artifact、wearable、cuffless BP
- 数据集：HealthRing
- 输入：reflective red/IR PPG、transmissive red/IR PPG、3-axis accelerometer
- 方法：Three synchronized cohorts: controlled stimuli, semi-free-living activities and treadmill running; RingTool provides signal-processing and DL baselines.
- 主要结果：54 adults; controlled+daily benchmarks: SBP/DBP MAE 12.98/7.64 mmHg, HR 5.33 bpm, RR 2.98 breaths/min, SpO2 1.72%.
- 校准：benchmark-dependent；Dataset paper does not establish a product calibration protocol.
- Subject split：dataset benchmark dependent；Downstream users must preserve subject separation.
- External validation：cross-cohort stress test；Motion cohort tests transfer, but BP benchmark is mainly controlled/daily.
- 可部署性：真实 wearable 数据价值高，BP 精度有限（research-enabler）
- 推理输入：ring PPG、accelerometer
- 泄露风险：medium；Benchmark MAE is not a consumer-device validation.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## Causal Decomposition of PPG Signals for Cuffless Blood Pressure Estimation

- 来源：[IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42268766/)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、EEMD、causal inference、interpretability
- 数据集：not_reported
- 输入：PPG
- 方法：EEMD decomposes PPG into IMFs; counterfactual exclusion and structural causal modeling select BP-relevant components; CNN/GBRT/PAT baselines are compared.
- 主要结果：IMF4–6 showed strongest causal association; CNN using IMF4+5 achieved SBP/DBP MAE 5.55/3.45 mmHg, improving 26.39%/17.86% over original PPG.
- 校准：unknown；Abstract does not describe a deployment calibration protocol.
- Subject split：unknown；Needs full-text protocol audit.
- External validation：false；No external cohort confirmed from abstract.
- 可部署性：算法上可 PPG-only，证据不足（promising-research）
- 推理输入：PPG、fixed EEMD/component-selection pipeline
- 泄露风险：medium；Causal language does not substitute for subject-disjoint and external validation.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
