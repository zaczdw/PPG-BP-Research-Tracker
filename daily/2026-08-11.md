## Evaluating cross-dataset transfer learning for photoplethysmography-based blood pressure estimation

- 来源：[Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-43409-8)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、cross-dataset transfer、personalization
- 数据集：MIMIC-III、VitalDB、Radboud wearable dataset
- 输入：PPG
- 方法：Compact BP-CRNN pretrained on a source dataset; last convolutional and fully connected layers are fine-tuned with limited target-subject data.
- 主要结果：Cross-dataset personalization on 200 patients achieved SBP/DBP MAE 3.36/1.81 mmHg, about 13% better than no transfer; external wearable-domain errors remained around SBP 10.5–10.9 and DBP 6.9 mmHg in the prior review.
- 校准：subject-specific fine-tuning；Needs limited target-user PPG with reference BP.
- Subject split：true；Evaluation is organized by target subjects; source and target datasets are distinct.
- External validation：true；Shows clear hospital-to-wearable domain gap.
- 可部署性：有条件可部署（conditional）
- 推理输入：PPG、stored personalized weights
- 泄露风险：low-to-medium；Strong deployment relevance, but personalized results cannot be read as calibration-free cross-person generalization.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
