## Cuffless hemodynamic monitoring with physics-informed machine learning models

- 来源：[Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72693-1)
- 主题：BioZ、cuffless BP、sPINN、physics-informed ML、smartwatch
- 数据集：PulseDB + synthetic BioZ、75 healthy participants、hypertension/CVD clinical cohorts、one-year pilot
- 输入：wrist BioZ resistance/reactance、space-time coordinates、optional anthropometric/anatomical metadata
- 方法：BioZ signal encoder plus signal-tagged PINN constrained by Navier–Stokes/elastic-tube physics predicts BP and radial/axial velocity fields.
- 主要结果：Cross-sectional r² ranges about 0.588–0.854 across cohorts; subject-specific models reached higher r². One-year pilot required daily recalibration to maintain performance.
- 校准：population model plus optional subject-specific/daily recalibration；Despite calibration-free framing, full text reports limited longitudinal generalization and daily recalibration in the pilot.
- Subject split：mixed/limited；Population-wide results are shown without test-exclusive patients; subject-specific results are not unseen-subject generalization.
- External validation：clinical feasibility cohorts；No established cuffless BP validation protocol yet.
- 可部署性：硬件可穿戴，算法纵向稳定性不足（promising-but-not-ready）
- 推理输入：wrist BioZ、stored model、optional demographics、possibly recent recalibration
- 泄露风险：high evaluation/generalization risk；Not necessarily classic target leakage, but evidence does not establish calibration-free unseen-user longitudinal deployment.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
