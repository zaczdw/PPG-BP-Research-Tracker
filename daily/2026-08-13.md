## Benchmarking and Enhancing PPG-Based Cuffless Blood Pressure Estimation Methods

- 来源：[BIOSIGNALS 2026](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2026/142454/)
- 主题：PPG、cuffless BP、benchmark、demographics、clinical criteria
- 数据集：NBPDB、MIMIC-III、VitalDB
- 输入：PPG、age、sex、BMI
- 方法：Standardized 101,453-segment benchmark from 1,103 healthy adults; compares multiple models and demographic fusion.
- 主要结果：No baseline model met AAMI/ISO numerical limits; demographic-enhanced MInception reduced error 23% to SBP/DBP MAE 4.75/2.90 mmHg.
- 校准：none reported；Demographics are covariates, not cuff calibration.
- Subject split：reported standardized benchmark；Full split manifest should be preserved in reuse.
- External validation：false；Derived from two public ICU/OR datasets, not consumer-device clinical validation.
- 可部署性：有助于公平比较，非产品验证（research-benchmark）
- 推理输入：PPG、age、sex、BMI
- 泄露风险：medium；Healthy-adult subset and demographic baselines may narrow target variance.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## AnyPPG: An ECG-Guided PPG Foundation Model Trained on Over 100,000 Hours of Recordings for Holistic Health Profiling

- 来源：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01747)
- 主题：PPG、foundation model、ECG-guided pretraining、wearable
- 数据集：six multi-source synchronized PPG-ECG datasets
- 输入：PPG at deployment、ECG only during pretraining
- 方法：Dual-modal alignment places synchronized PPG and ECG representations in a shared physiological space; downstream use can retain only the PPG encoder.
- 主要结果：Pretraining covers about 109,909 h from 58,796 subjects; reports average gains of 12.8% in regression and 9.1% in classification across 11 tasks.
- 校准：task-specific downstream adaptation；No cuff BP calibration protocol is established by the foundation-model paper.
- Subject split：reported across downstream datasets；Exact per-task split must be checked before BP claims.
- External validation：true；Broad health tasks, not a dedicated cuffless BP device validation.
- 可部署性：PPG-only 表征可部署（research-enabler）
- 推理输入：PPG
- 泄露风险：low for target leakage / medium for downstream split provenance；Do not treat hypertension classification or foundation-model gains as absolute SBP/DBP validation.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## Arterial blood pressure waveform reconstruction estimation from PPG using TCN–BiLSTM

- 来源：[American Heart Journal Plus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13375927/)
- 主题：PPG、ABP waveform reconstruction、cuffless BP、TCN、BiLSTM、target leakage
- 数据集：VitalDB、MIMIC-IV
- 输入：PPG、first derivative PPG、second derivative PPG
- 方法：8 s PPG windows (2 s stride) are mapped to normalized ABP waveforms by a TCN–BiLSTM with extrema-aware Huber loss.
- 主要结果：VitalDB reported SBP MAE/RMSE 3.61/5.24 and DBP 1.79/2.37 mmHg; external MIMIC-IV table reported about 4.6/1.9 mmHg MAE.
- 校准：none claimed；However, each test ABP window supplies its own mean and standard deviation for denormalization, which functions as unavailable target information.
- Subject split：true；VitalDB split 1,568/328/330 subjects; overlapping windows stay within subject partitions.
- External validation：true；External test without retraining is reported, but the same target-dependent preprocessing issue applies.
- 可部署性：按论文流程不可部署为 PPG-only 绝对 BP（not-deployable-as-reported）
- 推理输入：PPG、current true ABP window mean、current true ABP window standard deviation
- 泄露风险：critical；Subject split and external data do not cancel leakage caused by current-test ABP statistics and ABP-based test selection.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed

## PulseLM: A Foundation Dataset and Benchmark for PPG-Text Learning

- 来源：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03331)
- 主题：PPG、foundation dataset、PPG-text、question answering
- 数据集：15 public PPG sources
- 输入：PPG、text question
- 方法：Harmonized PPG segments paired with physiological question–answer tasks.
- 主要结果：About 1.31 million 10-second PPG segments and 3.15 million QA pairs across 12 physiological QA tasks.
- 校准：not applicable；Dataset/benchmark, not a cuffless BP calibration method.
- Subject split：varies-by-source/task；Must preserve subject IDs from each constituent dataset.
- External validation：false；Benchmark aggregation, not clinical BP validation.
- 可部署性：研究基础设施（research-enabler）
- 推理输入：PPG、task/question specification
- 泄露风险：medium；Aggregating many sources amplifies split-provenance risk.
- 审计状态：priority-reviewed
